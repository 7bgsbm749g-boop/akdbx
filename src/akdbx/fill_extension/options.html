akdbx settings

Experimental

Before an agent fills a card or address field, or captures a secret, akdbx colours it for a moment and takes a screenshot of the tab to check that nothing on the page covers it.

Drawbacks: needs screenshot access to all sites. The field flashes, each field takes about a second more, and the tab must stay in front.

Can fail: moving pages or displays that change colours can stop a safe fill. If fills stop for no clear reason, turn this off. Logins are not checked.
